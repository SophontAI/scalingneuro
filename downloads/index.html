First run

  1. Extract the complete bundle; keep libexec/dcm2niix beside the app.
  2. Open neuro-sync and complete the one-minute lab registration on first launch.
  3. Confirm that the scans are covered by your institution’s IRB, participant-consent, and data-use approvals; then select the DICOM export folder.

Source DICOMs never leave the machine. V1 uploads only native-space functional 4D NIfTI, an explicit metadata allowlist, and checksum/provenance manifests. Structural, diffusion, reference, derived, or ambiguous scans stay local.

The registration creates a private, revocable upload identity for your lab. It is not evidence of participant consent and cannot authorize an otherwise impermissible upload.

Every published bundle has SHA-256 checksums and SPDX/CycloneDX SBOMs. Beta packages may still carry UNSIGNED-PILOT or CODESIGNED-PILOT filenames until signing is configured; operating-system warnings are shown before download.
