What happens next

  1. Complete the one-minute lab registration directly in the terminal. Nothing opens in a browser.
  2. Confirm that the scans are covered by your institution’s IRB, participant-consent, and data-use approvals.
  3. Type, paste, or drag in the completed DICOM export-folder path. neuro-sync handles classification, local conversion, metadata minimization, QC, and resumable upload.

Source DICOMs never leave the machine. Only native-space functional 4D NIfTI, approved acquisition metadata, and checksum/provenance manifests upload. Structural, diffusion, reference, derived, and ambiguous scans stay local.

Manual downloads and release files
