First run

  1. Extract the complete bundle; keep libexec/dcm2niix beside the app.
  2. Open neuro-sync, paste the project invite once, and review the displayed contribution-policy version.
  3. Confirm that the scans are covered by your institution’s IRB, participant-consent, and data-use approvals; then select the DICOM export folder.

Source DICOMs never leave the machine. V1 uploads only native-space functional 4D NIfTI, an explicit metadata allowlist, and checksum/provenance manifests. Structural, diffusion, reference, derived, or ambiguous scans stay local.

An invite grants this workstation access to a pre-approved project. It is not evidence of participant consent and cannot authorize an otherwise impermissible upload.

Every published bundle has SHA-256 checksums and SPDX/CycloneDX SBOMs. Restricted builds are labeled UNSIGNED-PILOT or CODESIGNED-PILOT; warnings appear above before download.
