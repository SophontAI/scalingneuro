Functional EPI contribution policy

open-epi-3.0.0 · effective 31 July 2026

What neuro-sync shares

neuro-sync searches the selected folder for functional echo-planar imaging time series. Only series supported by standard EPI and temporal DICOM metadata and the local privacy checks are uploaded. Structural scans, localizers, diffusion, field maps, secondary captures, non-image objects, and ambiguous series stay on the workstation.

Your authority to contribute and license

By continuing, you confirm that you are the owner of the selected functional MRI data or are authorized by the owner and your institution to deidentify the data, share it publicly, and apply the CC0 dedication described below. You also confirm that you have every participant-consent, ethics, IRB, data-use, contractual, and institutional permission required for this public sharing. This confirmation does not replace any of those reviews or permissions.

CC0 public-domain dedication

When Scaling Neuro successfully receives an uploaded archive, you dedicate all copyright and related rights in that archive that you are authorized to grant to the public domain under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal. CC0 is irrevocable and permits anyone to copy, modify, distribute, and use the data for any purpose, including commercial purposes, without conditions.

CC0 applies only to rights you own or are authorized to grant. It does not make the data anonymous, waive participant privacy or publicity rights, or replace compliance with applicable law and ethical obligations.

What leaves the workstation

  • Functional EPI Pixel Data. Pixel Data is preserved in the rewritten DICOMs.
  • Acquisition metadata. A conservative allowlist retains fields needed to decode and interpret the acquisition.
  • Pseudonymous linkage. Patient, study, series, instance, and reference identities are replaced with site-scoped values.
  • Integrity evidence. The archive contains counts, policy versions, classifier evidence, and hashes.

What is removed locally

Direct identifiers, dates and times, clinical and administrative fields, institution, station and operator identity, descriptions, comments, source paths and filenames, overlays, graphics, and unreviewed private metadata are removed before transfer. The rewritten files are recursively audited before an archive can be uploaded.

Storage and use

One deidentified DICOM archive per selected series is stored in Cloudflare R2. Researchers can request access, download the DICOM archives, and choose their own conversion and preprocessing workflow. Scaling Neuro does not run conversion, preprocessing, or analysis. Access controls and the archive privacy agreement are separate from the CC0 dedication and do not narrow the CC0 license.

Withdrawal

A lab may request workstation revocation or removal of Scaling Neuro's hosted copy by contacting admin@sophont.med with the pseudonymous upload ID from its local report. Removing a hosted copy does not revoke CC0 or retrieve copies already received by others.

Stopping is safe. Cancelling before the authorization prompt uploads nothing. neuro-sync prepare also uploads nothing, allowing a lab to inspect or edit the local deidentified DICOMs before a separate upload command. Interrupted work resumes only when the same folder command is run again.
