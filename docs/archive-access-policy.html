Scaling Neuro

Archive data-use policy

archive-access-1.0.0 · effective 31 July 2026

Who must follow this policy

This policy applies to every person who receives or uses data from the Scaling Neuro archive, including approved researchers, their collaborators, Scaling Neuro administrators, and project staff. An approved researcher is responsible for ensuring that anyone using data under their access follows these conditions.

Authority to receive and use data

Before requesting access, you must have all institutional, ethical, legal, contractual, and other permissions required for you to receive and use the deidentified data for secondary research. Scaling Neuro approval does not replace your institution's review or authorize a use that your institution does not permit.

CC0 and privacy

Archives received under the current contribution policy are dedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal. CC0 permits unrestricted reuse of copyright and related rights, but does not make participants anonymous or waive privacy and publicity rights. These access conditions are a separate agreement for researchers who receive data through Scaling Neuro.

No identification or reidentification

You must not attempt to identify or reidentify any participant represented in the archive. You must not link archive data with other information for the purpose of discovering a participant's identity, and you must not help anyone else do so.

No participant contact

You must not contact or attempt to contact a participant based on archive data. If a participant's identity becomes known or is suspected, do not use that information or contact the participant. Secure the affected data and promptly notify admin@sophont.med.

Personal access and collaborators

Your access token is personal and must not be shared. You may allow collaborators at your approved lab to work with downloaded data only after they understand and agree to these same conditions. You remain responsible for activity under your access and for promptly reporting loss or misuse of your token.

Enforcement and policy changes

Access may be denied or revoked for a violation, suspected violation, or failure to accept the current policy. Material policy changes require a new, version-specific acceptance before access continues.

Deidentified does not mean anonymous. Scaling Neuro removes identifying DICOM metadata locally, but preserves the functional EPI Pixel Data. These access conditions are an additional privacy safeguard and do not guarantee that identification is impossible.
