Make required data sharing automatic.

Your grant’s Data Management and Sharing Plan says what must be shared, when, and where. Scaling Neuro prepares approved fMRI—with privacy checks, provenance, and hashes—for repository deposit and progress reporting.

        03 — For every new session
        
Point it at one folder. Let every future scan count.

        

          Keep the scanner workflow. Paste one terminal command once; neuro-sync then finds approved functional EPI scans, converts and checks them locally, and resumes until every verified bundle is committed.
        


        

          One terminal commandNo admin, Python, or DockerFails closed
        


        

          

            
            
            
          

          

            
# macOS or Linux — installs in your home folder and opens neuro-sync
$ curl -fsSL https://scalingneuro.com/install.sh | sh
# Windows PowerShell: irm https://scalingneuro.com/install.ps1 | iex
✓ Correct client + pinned dcm2niix downloaded and SHA-256 verified
✓ One-minute lab registration on first launch
✓ Private upload identity: your lab
✓ Choose the folder containing this session's DICOMs
✓ Discover → convert → minimize metadata → QC → upload
✓ Safe to stop: conversion and multipart upload resume locally

            
# after the one-time guided lab registration
$ neuro-sync upload ./new_session_dicoms

[scan]   6 DICOM series discovered
  ↑ functional EPI   2.0mm  480 vol   committed
  ↑ functional EPI   1.6mm  610 vol   committed
  — T1w / DWI / SBRef / uncertain series      held locally
[meta]   scanner · field · TR · TE · geometry · timing retained
[archive] native NIfTI + sidecar + manifest + SHA-256   complete

            
local    source DICOMs remain unchanged on the lab's machine
voxels   native-space .nii.gz; no registration, masking, or resampling
metadata explicit acquisition allowlist; unknown keys are dropped
identity names, IDs, dates, UIDs, sites, paths, and free text never upload
linkage  site-scoped HMAC subject/session/series pseudonyms only
proof    converter version, QC decisions, and compressed + raw hashes
hold     structural, diffusion, field maps, references, and ambiguity stay local
access   device credentials can only upload parts to a server-created session
