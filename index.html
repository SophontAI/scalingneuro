The scale opportunity

Just 25 centers could match every public fMRI dataset ever released.

Every public fMRI dataset ever released adds up to about 32,200 hours of scanning. A single academic MRI center collects about 25 hours each week, and nearly all of it stays on local drives. If 25 centers contribute the scans they already collect, one year equals the entire public archive.

Sources + assumptions

The public baseline is the roughly 32,200 recorded fMRI hours estimated across freely accessible archives in NeuroAI for AI Safety. The network bar is a planning scenario: 25 centers × 25 hours/week × 52 weeks = 32,500 acquired hours. The shareable total would be lower after consent and privacy gates. The dotted curve extends the strict data-scaling trend reported in CortexMAE; it is not a performance projection.

For NIH- and NSF-funded studies

Make responsible data sharing easier to execute.

Since 2023, NIH has required a Data Management and Sharing Plan with every new award, and NSF sets similar expectations. Scaling Neuro captures institution-approved MR at the source and creates a verifiable archive record—privacy checks, provenance, hashes, and processing status—for later repository preparation and progress reporting.

Approved MRI→Verified archive→Repository preparation

Scaling Neuro supports—but does not replace—participant consent, institutional review, an approved sharing plan, or a repository’s submission process.

How it works

One folder. One durable archive pipeline.

The lab decides what is authorized. neuro-sync then discovers, protects, and preserves every supported MR Image series without changing the source folder or requiring labels, BIDS curation, or cloud setup.

01 · Authority

Approve

The lab confirms the folder and scanner-native pixels are institutionally authorized.

02 · Discovery

Inventory

Read the export without changing it; group every supported MR Image series.

03 · Privacy

Protect

Remove identifying metadata locally while preserving exact Pixel Data and acquisition context.

04 · Receipt

Archive

Upload one resumable series archive at a time to R2; a durable receipt ends workstation work.

05 · Cluster

Verify

Re-audit every archive on Sophont; derive validated NIfTI only for confirmed functional EPI.

Current processing boundary: this release converts and validates functional EPI in native space. It does not yet perform motion correction, distortion correction, registration, normalization, BIDS curation, or structural defacing.

Self-service beta

Point it at one folder. Make eligible scans count.

Keep the scanner workflow. Install once, then run neuro-sync whenever an authorized export folder is ready. It finds every supported MR Image series, removes identifying DICOM metadata locally, and uploads each series with resumable checksums. The workstation is done when transfer receives its durable receipt; independent verification and EPI-only derivation continue on the research cluster.

Scanner make, model, and software are provenance, not a whitelist. Functional, structural, diffusion, field-map, reference, localizer, and derived MR use the same standard-DICOM integrity and metadata-privacy gates. Secondary Capture, non-image DICOM, malformed series, declared burned-in annotation, and other unsupported privacy conditions stay local with a report code.
