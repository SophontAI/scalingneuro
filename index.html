Academic-led infrastructure to scale open neuroimaging data

Brain models need scale. The scans already exist.

neuro-sync gives academic labs one simple way to contribute approved functional MRI. Point it at a DICOM folder and it finds the EPI series, removes identifying metadata locally, and syncs the deidentified DICOMs to a shared archive.

The scale opportunity

Just 25 centers could match every public fMRI dataset ever released.

Freely accessible archives contain an estimated 32,200 hours of fMRI. A planning scenario of 25 academic centers each acquiring 25 hours per week would produce about the same amount in one year, before consent and privacy filtering.

Sources and assumptions

The public baseline is the estimate across freely accessible archives in NeuroAI for AI Safety. The network bar is 25 centers × 25 hours/week × 52 weeks. The shareable total would be lower after institutional, consent, and privacy review. The dotted curve extends the data-scaling trend reported in CortexMAE; it is not a performance projection.

How it works

Share approved fMRI from your lab.

01 · Workstation

Find

Walk one folder recursively and identify functional EPI time series from DICOM metadata.

02 · Local privacy

Protect

Remove direct identifiers, dates, paths, free text, and unsafe private metadata locally. Preserve Pixel Data.

03 · Shared archive

Sync

Upload one deidentified DICOM archive per EPI series with resumable transfers and content hashes.

04 · Participating labs

Use

Download the DICOMs and preprocess them with the tools and compute your lab already uses.

Functional EPI only. This scope is deliberate: functional EPI is comparatively low resolution and typically lacks the fine-grained facial anatomy found in structural MRI, avoiding the defacing step normally used for structural images. Structural scans, localizers, diffusion, field maps, secondary captures, and ambiguous series stay on the workstation. Identifying metadata is still removed locally; Scaling Neuro does not convert or preprocess the scans.

Install and contribute

Point it at one folder. Make eligible scans count.

Register the workstation once, then run neuro-sync whenever an institutionally authorized DICOM export is ready. Rerun the same folder after an interruption and completed work is reused.

One terminal command No Python or Docker Fails closed DICOM in, DICOM out 
# macOS or Linux
$ curl -fsSL https://scalingneuro.com/install.sh | sh
✓ neuro-sync 0.5.0 downloaded and SHA-256 verified

# One command for each approved scanner export
$ neuro-sync /path/to/dicom-folder
✓ One-time lab registration stays in the terminal
✓ Interrupted work resumes from the same folder command

Save it in your shell, then list the archive:

export SCALING_NEURO_ACCESS_TOKEN='PASTE_TOKEN'
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $SCALING_NEURO_ACCESS_TOKEN" \
  https://scalingneuro.com/v1/archive

curl -L -H "Authorization: Bearer $SCALING_NEURO_ACCESS_TOKEN" \
  DOWNLOAD_URL -o series.dicom.tar.zst
